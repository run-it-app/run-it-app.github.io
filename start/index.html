RUN IT QUICK START

Write your workout the way you'd text it — in Hebrew or English — and it lands on your Garmin watch, structured, with every pace on screen. Run It sends it to your free Intervals.icu calendar, which syncs planned workouts to Garmin Connect and your watch.

1 · Once, before you start

1

Free Intervals.icu account at intervals.icu.

2

Link Garmin: Intervals.icu → Settings → Connections → Garmin Connect, and tick Upload planned workouts.

Planned workouts reach the watch up to about 7 days ahead, the next time it syncs with Garmin Connect.

2 · Install

Run It is invite only. Your invite message has the Android link, the iPhone address and your invite code.

Android

Install the app from the link in your invite message (ask whoever invited you). Updates then arrive by themselves.

iPhone

Open run-it-app.github.io/app in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen.

3 · Set up (one minute)

1

Invite code — type the code from your invite message (capitals and dashes don't matter).

2

Connect with Intervals.icu, sign in and tap Allow. (Or paste an API key: Intervals.icu → Settings → Developer Settings.)

3

Garmin check — Run It shows whether Garmin is linked and planned uploads are on.

4

Your defaults — warm-up, cool-down, the jog between reps, strides. Used when you don't write them; change any time in Settings.

4 · Write a workout

Type, check the preview, tap Send to watch. One line per part; a first line with just a number is the day's total.

8*1000@4:15

8 × 1 km at 4:15, a 300 m jog between reps.

חימום2*800@4:1510*400@4:10שחרור

4 km warm-up · 2 × 800 m at 4:15 · 10 × 400 m at 4:10 (jogs between) · 2 km cool-down.

36פעמיים 10 קמ על 4:35

A 36 km day: 4 km warm-up · 10 km at 4:35 · 10 km easy · 10 km at 4:35 · 2 km cool-down.

31 ק״מ3 סטים של 4 ק״מ1. 4:452. 4:403. 4:35

A pace for each set, with easy running between, filling the 31 km.

שעתיים קל4 ק״מ על קצב 5:00-4:40

2 hours easy with the 4 km in the middle: 50:20 · 4 km · 50:20.

Good to know

  • Bare numbers: 100 and up are metres (2000). Under 100 follow the day's total (km or minutes); with no total, under 10 is km and 10–99 is minutes. With קל it's always km (12 קל = 12 km).
  • Pace is a note, not a target: the watch shows Pace "4:15" on each step; it won't beep at you.
  • AUTO lengths (warm-up, cool-down, the jog between reps) can be tapped in the preview and changed.
  • Strides: switch them on under the preview (default 4 × 120 m, after the warm-up).
  • Another day: tap the date chip.

5 · Write a week

Start each day with its name, in Hebrew or English. Run It switches to week mode by itself; pick the week (weeks start on Sunday) and send them all at once.

ראשון 12 קלשלישי5 פעמים 1000 על 4:00חמישי15 קללהכניס סט של 3 פעמים:1 ק״מ על 4:45500 מ׳ על 5:30

Three workouts for next week. Tap a day to check its steps, change its AUTO lengths or add strides for that day.

6 · Your workouts

  • Workouts (top of the screen) shows the week and your history — including workouts made in Intervals.icu.
  • Tap one for its steps, or use ⋯ / long-press: Edit, Repeat today, Delete. Delete this week clears a whole week.
  • Deleting removes it from Intervals.icu. If it already reached your watch, delete it there too.

7 · Share with a friend

Tap Share to send a link. Your friend opens it in their Run It and sends it to their own watch. Friends without Run It need an invite first.

8 · If something doesn't read right

Run It never guesses: anything it can't read is highlighted and nothing is sent. Copy the text and send it to whoever invited you — every real example becomes a new rule.
