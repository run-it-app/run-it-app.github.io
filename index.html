How it works

  • Run It turns your text into a structured workout on your phone.
  • It sends it to your free Intervals.icu calendar.
  • Intervals.icu syncs planned workouts to Garmin Connect, and your watch picks them up on its next sync.

Invite only

Run It is in a private beta, on Android and iPhone. If you were invited, the message you got has the links and your invite code.
