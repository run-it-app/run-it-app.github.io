Run It

PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Summary

Run It has no servers and no accounts of its own. It doesn't collect, store or sell your data. Everything it does happens on your phone and between your phone and Intervals.icu.

What the app stores on your phone

  • Your Intervals.icu access: either the sign-in token from "Connect with Intervals.icu" or a personal API key you paste in. In the Android app it is kept in the phone's encrypted secure storage. In the iPhone web app it is kept in the browser's storage for this site on that phone. Either way it is only ever sent to intervals.icu.
  • Your workouts and settings: the text of workouts you send, edits to defaults, strides settings and your default values, so you can reopen and edit them. Stored only on your phone.

What the app sends to Intervals.icu

When you send, edit or delete a workout, the app calls the Intervals.icu API directly from your phone to create, update or delete that planned workout on your calendar. To show your week and history it reads your planned workouts, and during setup it reads your Garmin connection settings. Intervals.icu's handling of that data is covered by its own privacy policy. Getting workouts onto your watch is done by Intervals.icu and Garmin Connect, under their policies.

Sharing a workout

If you share a workout, the link contains the workout text after a "#" in the address. That part of a link is never sent to this website's server; it is read only by the page in the recipient's browser, or by Run It on their phone.

What we don't do

  • No analytics, tracking, ads or third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • No access to your location, contacts, photos or health data.
  • No data is collected by the developer.

Removing your data

Uninstalling Run It removes everything it stored on your phone (for the web app: remove it from the Home Screen and clear this site's data in Safari settings). To revoke its access to Intervals.icu, remove Run It under Intervals.icu → Settings → Connected apps, or delete the API key there.
